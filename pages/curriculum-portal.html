KLI FAMILY GOVERNANCE PROGRAMS

Family Governance Curriculum

The Family Governance Curriculum is a structured educational program from the Office of the Fiduciary covering family trust formation, fiduciary duties, recordkeeping, succession, and multi-generational governance. This page provides a general program overview. The complete curriculum, including full lesson content and applied exercises, is available to authenticated members of the KLI Member Portal.

PROGRAM OVERVIEW
What This Program Covers

The Family Governance Curriculum introduces the doctrine behind trust formation, fiduciary obligations, administrative recordkeeping, trustee succession, beneficiary rights, and multi-generational governance continuity. It is organized into 8 structured modules for members progressing through the KLI educational pathway.

MODULES: 8 SECTIONS

INTENDED AUDIENCE
Who This Program Is For
AUD-001

Family Trust Principals

Settlors, trustees, and family members establishing or administering a family governance structure.

AUD-002

Successor Trustees

Individuals preparing to assume fiduciary responsibility for continuity and administration.

AUD-003

Governance Participants

KLI members building foundational and applied literacy in trust and fiduciary governance.

LEARNING OUTCOMES
General Learning Outcomes
OUT-001

Trust Formation Literacy

Understand the general elements and purpose of a family trust structure.

OUT-002

Fiduciary Duties

Understand the general duties of loyalty, care, and impartiality owed by a trustee.

OUT-003

Recordkeeping & Continuity

Understand why administrative records and succession planning matter for continuity.

OUT-004

Beneficiary Rights

Understand the general information, accounting, and communication rights of beneficiaries.
